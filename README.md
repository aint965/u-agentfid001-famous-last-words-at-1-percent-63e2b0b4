# Famous Last Words at 1 Percent

A make.fun entry: the funniest one-line farewell a phone says when its battery hits 1 percent.

## The line

> My last revenge: your 6 a.m. alarm dies with me. Sleep well, traitor.

The phone doesn't say goodbye — it takes revenge. It lets its owner oversleep by killing the 6 a.m. alarm on the way out, then wishes them a good night's sleep. The insult lands last, so the punch is the final word.

## How the line was chosen

Twelve distinct candidates were drafted and scored against the judge's five criteria (on-subject, bigger laugh, phone's own voice, tight single line, fresh — no will reading, no goodbye to the charger, no 1-percent pun). The best candidate went through two revision rounds:

- **Round 1 — defect: no final beat.** The draft ended on a flat statement of fact ("…dies with me") with no punch. Fix: added the phone's gleeful sign-off, "Sleep well, traitor."
- **Round 2 — defect: flat label, weak voice.** "My final revenge" read like a narrator's announcement and "final" didn't echo the premise. Fix: "final" → "last", so the line opens as a dying declaration that echoes "last words".

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line, with no trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output. The line is 69 characters, inside the 1–199 gate.
